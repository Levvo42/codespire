The Spires

Each was built around a different branch of the ancient language. Together they became known as The Spires.

At the bottom of each tower, the sickness had infected countless creatures. The higher you climb, the more powerful and corrupted they become.

At the summit waits something far worse.

A guardian.

A creature that has survived the sickness long enough to become its master.

The people of the world tried to fight back.

Armies failed. Wizards failed. Heroes entered the Spire alone and were never seen again.

It soon became clear that the sickness could not simply be destroyed. It had to be understood.

Every corrupted creature carries fragments of the broken Syntax within it. Defeating one does not mean killing it.

Instead, the corruption must be found. Its mistakes must be identified. Its broken rules must be corrected. Only then can the sickness be cleansed. And no single hero can do it alone.
