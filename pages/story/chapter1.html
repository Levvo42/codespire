The sickness

At first, the changes were small.

A word misplaced here.

A symbol missing there.

A command that should have worked, but didn't.

Then the sickness began spreading.

Creatures became twisted versions of themselves. Spells behaved unpredictably. Ancient guardians turned against the people they had once protected.

Even the land itself began to fracture as the rules holding reality together became corrupted.

And at the center of it all, something began to rise.

A tower.

Then another.

And another.
