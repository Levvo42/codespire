Prologue:

For millennia, the world had stood upon the foundations of a forgotten language.

Long before kings raised their castles and before the first adventurers ventured into the wild, the ancient peoples discovered that reality itself was written in Syntax.

Every spell, every creature, every mountain and every living thing was bound together by rules older than magic.

For thousands of years, those rules held.

Until something went wrong.

No one knows where the sickness began.

Some say it came from beneath the earth. Others claim it was born from a spell that should never have been cast. Ancient texts speak only of a single name:

The Syntax Sickness.

Legends say that somewhere beyond the highest tower lies the source of the Syntax Sickness.

Some believe it is a monster, some believe it is a forgotten spell.

Others believe it is something far olderm, but no one knows the truth.

The people of the world have waited for millennia for someone to find it.

Now, the gates of the Spire stand open and the monsters are waiting.

And the sickness is spreading.
